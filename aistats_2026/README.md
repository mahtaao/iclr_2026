# AISTATS 2026 paper workspace

`main.tex` is a blank paper skeleton. It contains no text from the existing draft.

`aistats2026.sty` and `fancyhdr.sty` come from the official [AISTATS 2026 Paper Pack](https://aistats.org/aistats2026/AISTATS2026PaperPack.zip). The original sample and supplement source files are kept in `template/` for reference.

The section headings are placeholders; the paper structure can change after reviewing the new plots and results.
